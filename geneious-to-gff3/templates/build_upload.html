Upload the Geneious file of one gene

One job builds one gene. If the gene has splice variants, select all of their files at once (Ctrl-click on Windows, Cmd-click on a Mac), or keep all variants in one Geneious file. You can also add variants on the next page.

What the file must contain

  • One nucleotide sequence per transcript: the genomic sequence (exons with introns) from the first to the last base of the transcript.
  • Annotations of type exon, one per exon, including the UTR parts. Their names (Ex1, Ex2, ...) and their order do not matter.
  • For a protein-coding transcript, one annotation of type CDS from the A of the start codon to the last base of the stop codon. It may span the introns; a joined CDS also works.
  • For a non-coding transcript (a lncRNA, for example) simply leave the CDS out: annotate the exons only. The next page then asks what kind of transcript it is.
  • Nothing else is needed. Other annotation types (for example "extracted region") are ignored and listed on the next page.

The gene should read left to right. If you keep a minus-strand gene in chromosome orientation instead, make the annotation arrows point right to left; the tool then turns the sequence around by itself.
